I just migrated my personal website to SvelteKit. It's the third time I rewrite my website, previously I always use React and Next.js because it's the first framework I learned and I was kind of lazy to learn a new one. This time I decide to use Svelte because it seems much lightweight than the bulky Next.js app.

Svelte is quite easy to learn since most of the concept are just plain html and javascript.

Most of the pain during the migration is not about learning the new tool but to rewrite all the old code. Comparing to understand the trash I write few years ago, it's actually easier to just start over.
